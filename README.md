STREET FIGHTER ALPHA / ZERO 2 GOLD+ RECOMP PROJECT

After what felt like an endless amount of bug hunting, I have narrowed down what I could for this build. 
Things are not perfect, so please don't expect that from this game; it wasn't perfect in 1996. 
Custom, bespoke game values will be adjusted as players explore and break things, of course. 
All game modes and functions added to the game are OPTIONAL, and G+ mechanics can be disabled for the vanilla experience. 

With that said, I happily present to you my extension of a game that is very important to me.  

Here in the repo, you'll find everything you need to play except for ROMs.

The required ROMs to play are:

sfa2.zip
sfa2u.zip 

They are read directly from the `rom/` folder. 

Manuals, or FIELD GUIDES, will (eventually) be available in PDF and text-only markdown formats. 

Until then, v0.3 of the current FIELD GUIDES are HTML-sided here: https://sfa2gp.neocities.org/

The current available languages are:

*ENGLISH (US)*

*SPANISH (US)*

*JAPANESE*

Future languages are planned, but have not yet been implemented, so forgive that. 

Please report any and all bugs you can find, and I will do my best to get them patched for the next build. 
If it's a bug that's fun, it may even be kept as a feature (because, you know, why not)!

Above all, enjoy. 

Play to your heart's content.
